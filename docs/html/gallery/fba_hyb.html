FBA-Hyb¶

Demonstrates. A hybrid dynamic-FBA reaction module: two small MLPs predict a glucose-uptake rate and a flux-balance-analysis objective from the current state, a frozen, pole-free surrogate converts that into real metabolic rates, and ReactionOutputs.auxiliary exposes what the model believes the cell’s metabolic allocation is doing over the batch.

Dynamic flux-balance analysis (dFBA) normally means solving a real linear program, max c^T v subject to S.v = 0 and flux bounds, at every integration step. That is not something JAX can differentiate through or vmap/jit the way it does a neural network, and hybrax.train has no LP solver in its training loop. The fix, from Gotsmy & Guillén-Gosálbez’s FBA-Hyb [1]: fit a closed-form, pole-free surrogate of the FBA solution once, offline (10,000 real linear programs solved outside the training loop), then embed that surrogate directly inside an ordinary UserReactionModule. No LP solve ever happens during training.

The surrogate here is fit fresh against e_coli_core.xml [2], a small, real, published teaching model (95 reactions, 72 metabolites), not a proprietary genome-scale network: see Knowledge below for the full, reproducible chain. See PLS-dFBA for this page’s sibling, which builds on the same surrogate with a product-forming, media-blend-aware extension.

 57def _init_xavier_zero_bias(model, key):

AVG_QG/AVG_N are the scaler constants from the offline fit; q_glc is analytic (the glucose-uptake bound fixes it exactly), the other three fluxes are the fitted pole-free rational form: q = qG · (A1·n)(A2·n) / ((pos(B1·n)+d)(pos(B2·n)+d)), pos(B) = 0.5(B + sqrt(B² + 1.5)). Every denominator factor is at least d > 0 for any input, by construction: the surrogate cannot blow up inside an ODE solver’s adjoint however far the reaction module’s own predictions wander, which is exactly what a real LP solve would do if you tried to differentiate through it.

Two small MLPs predict qG (glucose uptake) and the FBA objective weights (n_X, n_M, n_A) (biomass, ATP maintenance, acetate) from the current state, each squashed through _bounded_softplus to stay inside the range the surrogate was actually fit over. n_S (succinate) is fixed at 0.0: this page has no deliberate product, only real E. coli overflow metabolism (growth versus maintenance versus acetate secretion), the same trade-off the surrogate was trained on. The predicted weights go out through auxiliary, which hybrax.train threads straight into predictions.csv.

What the model believes is happening¶

aux_n_weights_0/1/2 are ReactionOutputs.auxiliary["n_weights"], written out with no extra plumbing beyond what every other page already gets. The trend is genuinely learned, not told: the model was never given time as an input, only state, yet n_A rises steadily over the batch (real overflow metabolism intensifying as glucose depletes and biomass accumulates), inferred purely from the state it was shown.

How the surrogate was fit¶

10,000 real parsimonious-FBA solves on e_coli_core.xml [2], Latin-hypercube-sampled over (qG, n_X, n_M, n_A, n_S):

01_generate_fba_data.py
  1"""Generate FBA training data for a fresh surrogate fit on e_coli_core.
  2
  3Run once, offline, not part of docs_rebuild.sh: solving 10,000 real linear
  4programs takes a couple of minutes and needs `cobra` (already in the same
  5environment bp-docs builds against, but not something every doc-build should
  6pay for). Its output, `01_obj_fba_data.csv`, feeds `02_fit_surrogate.py`,
  7whose output is the literal `surrogate_fba` body hardcoded into
  8`fba_hyb_custom.py` and `pls_dfba_custom.py`. Kept here, next to those files,
  9so the whole chain from real FBA solves to the two gallery pages' frozen
 10surrogate is reproducible, per bp-docs' own "generated by a documented,
 11deterministic script" rule -- it just doesn't run on every build.
 12
 13Combined objective: max  n_X*biomass + n_M*ATPM + n_A*acetate_secretion
 14                         + n_S*succinate_secretion

 16solves pFBA for each. n_S is included for both pages (page 1's reaction module

 19Mirrors the pattern in Gotsmy & Guillen-Gosalbez's FBA-Hyb method
 20(bioRxiv:10.64898/2026.04.22.720062v1), adapted to e_coli_core (Orth, Fleming

 61def build_model(qG, n_X, n_M, n_A, n_S, tmp_model, xxx_equiv, atp_equiv, ace_equiv, suc_equiv):
 62    reaction = cobra.Reaction("combined_obj")
 63    reaction.name = "combined_obj_function"
 64    reaction.lower_bound = 0.0
 65    reaction.upper_bound = 1000.0
 66    reaction.add_metabolites({
 67        xxx_equiv: -n_X,
 68        atp_equiv: -n_M,
 69        ace_equiv: -n_A,
 70        suc_equiv: -n_S,
 71    })
 72    tmp_model.add_reactions([reaction])
 73    tmp_model.objective = "combined_obj"
 74    tmp_model.objective_direction = "max"
 75    tmp_model.reactions.get_by_id(GLC_RID).bounds = (-qG, -qG)
 76    return tmp_model

 79if __name__ == "__main__":
 80    sampler = qmc.LatinHypercube(d=5, seed=20260813)
 81    n_samples = 10_000
 82    norm_sample = sampler.random(n_samples)
 83    # dims: qG, n_X, n_M, n_A, n_S
 84    scaled_sample = qmc.scale(norm_sample, [0.5, 0.0, 0.0, 0.0, 0.0], [20.0, 2.0, 2.0, 2.0, 2.0])
 85
 86    model, xxx_equiv, atp_equiv, ace_equiv, suc_equiv = load_model()
 87
 88    rows = []
 89    n_fail = 0
 90    for qG, n_X, n_M, n_A, n_S in scaled_sample:
 91        with model as tmp:

 93            try:

 96                    np.array([qG, n_X, n_M, n_A, n_S]),
 97                    sol.fluxes.values.flatten(),
 98                ]))
 99            except Exception:
100                n_fail += 1
101
102    with model as tmp:
103        tmp = build_model(1, 1, 1, 1, 1, tmp, xxx_equiv, atp_equiv, ace_equiv, suc_equiv)
104        columns = ["qG", "n_X", "n_M", "n_A", "n_S"] + [r.id for r in tmp.reactions]

Then a fresh pole-free rational fit against that data (multi-restart L-BFGS, method from FBA-Hyb [1]), with a boundedness certificate checked before acceptance: the metric that actually predicts training stability, since a surrogate that looks perfect on held-out data can still spike in the sparse gaps of the sampling box and blow up an ODE adjoint:

02_fit_surrogate.py
  1"""Fit a pole-free rational surrogate for e_coli_core's FBA solution.
  2
  3Run once, offline, after 01_generate_fba_data.py. Method from Gotsmy &
  4Guillen-Gosalbez's FBA-Hyb (bioRxiv:10.64898/2026.04.22.720062v1): a
  5symbolic-regression-discovered rational functional form, its coefficients
  6re-fit here directly against fresh FBA data via multi-restart L-BFGS
  7(skipping symbolic regression itself, since the functional form is already
  8known):
  9
 10    q_i = qG * (A1.n)(A2.n) / ( (pos(B1.n)+d) * (pos(B2.n)+d) )
 11    pos(B) = 0.5*(B + sqrt(B^2 + c)),  c = 1.5
 12
 13Every denominator factor is >= d > 0 for all inputs, by construction: the
 14surrogate is pole-free, so it cannot blow up inside an ODE solver's adjoint
 15however far the trained reaction module's own predictions wander. This
 16script's boundedness certificate (below) checks that directly, over the full
 17sampling box, before accepting a fit.
 18
 19n = (n_X, n_M, n_A, n_S). q_glc is analytic (= -qG exactly, by construction of
 2001_generate_fba_data.py's glucose-bound fixing). Fitted fluxes: biomass, ATPM,
 21EX_ac_e, EX_succ_e. Its output, 02_surrogate_body.txt, is the literal
 22`surrogate_fba` body pasted into fba_hyb_custom.py and pls_dfba_custom.py.
 23"""
 24import sys
 25import numpy as np
 26import pandas as pd
 27import scipy.optimize
 28import jax
 29import jax.numpy as jnp
 30jax.config.update("jax_enable_x64", True)
 31
 32C_RAMP = 1.5
 33VALIDATION_SPLIT = 0.1
 34N_RESTARTS = int(sys.argv[1]) if len(sys.argv) > 1 else 40
 35
 36RID = {
 37    "qX": "BIOMASS_Ecoli_core_w_GAM",
 38    "qM": "ATPM",
 39    "qA": "EX_ac_e",
 40    "qS": "EX_succ_e",
 41}
 42FIT_FLUXES = ["qX", "qM", "qA", "qS"]
 43DELTA = {"qX": 0.05, "qM": 0.05, "qA": 0.05, "qS": 0.05}
 44
 45
 46def load():
 47    df = pd.read_csv("01_obj_fba_data.csv", index_col=0)
 48    avg = df[["qG", "n_X", "n_M", "n_A", "n_S"]].mean(axis=0)
 49    AVG_QG = float(avg["qG"])
 50    AVG_N = np.array([avg["n_X"], avg["n_M"], avg["n_A"], avg["n_S"]], float)
 51
 52    qG_scaled = df["qG"].values / AVG_QG
 53    n_scaled = df[["n_X", "n_M", "n_A", "n_S"]].values / AVG_N
 54    Y = {k: df[v].values.astype(float) for k, v in RID.items()}
 55    return df, AVG_QG, AVG_N, qG_scaled, n_scaled, Y
 56
 57
 58def canonical_split(n_rows):
 59    key = jax.random.PRNGKey(13)
 60    ID = jax.random.choice(key, n_rows, shape=(int(n_rows * VALIDATION_SPLIT),), replace=False)
 61    ID = np.asarray(ID)
 62    valid = np.zeros(n_rows, bool)
 63    valid[ID] = True
 64    return ~valid, valid
 65
 66
 67def pos(B):
 68    return 0.5 * (B + jnp.sqrt(B * B + C_RAMP))
 69
 70
 71def aff(w, N):
 72    return N @ w[:4] + w[4]
 73
 74
 75def predict(Z, g, N, d):
 76    A1, A2 = aff(Z[0:5], N), aff(Z[5:10], N)
 77    B1, B2 = aff(Z[10:15], N), aff(Z[15:20], N)
 78    return g * A1 * A2 / ((pos(B1) + d) * (pos(B2) + d))
 79
 80
 81def r2(y, p):
 82    t = np.sum((y - y.mean()) ** 2)
 83    return 1.0 - np.sum((y - p) ** 2) / t if t > 0 else 1.0
 84
 85
 86def nmae(y, p):
 87    m = np.mean(np.abs(y))
 88    return np.mean(np.abs(y - p)) / m if m > 0 else 0.0
 89
 90
 91def fit_flux(g, N, y, tr, d, n_restarts, seed):
 92    ytr = jnp.asarray(y[tr]); gtr = jnp.asarray(g[tr]); Ntr = jnp.asarray(N[tr])
 93    ay = jnp.abs(ytr)
 94    sc = float(np.mean(np.abs(y[tr]))) + 1e-12
 95    floor = max(float(np.percentile(np.abs(y[tr]), 5)), 0.02 * sc)
 96    eps2 = (0.01 * sc) ** 2
 97    REL_WEIGHT = 1.0
 98
 99    def loss(Z):
100        p = predict(Z, gtr, Ntr, d)
101        sh = jnp.sqrt((ytr - p) ** 2 + eps2)
102        return jnp.mean(sh) / sc + REL_WEIGHT * jnp.mean(sh / (ay + floor))
103
104    vgj = jax.jit(jax.value_and_grad(loss))
105
106    def vg(Z):
107        v, gg = vgj(jnp.asarray(Z))
108        return float(v), np.asarray(gg, float)
109
110    rng = np.random.default_rng(seed)
111    best, best_loss = None, np.inf
112
113    def run(Z0):
114        nonlocal best, best_loss
115        try:
116            r = scipy.optimize.minimize(
117                vg, Z0, jac=True, method="L-BFGS-B",
118                options={"maxiter": 3000, "maxfun": 6000, "ftol": 1e-15, "gtol": 1e-12},
119            )
120        except Exception:
121            return
122        if np.isfinite(r.fun) and r.fun < best_loss:
123            best_loss, best = r.fun, r.x
124
125    num = lambda: np.concatenate([rng.normal(0, 1, 4), [rng.normal(0, 0.3)]])
126    den = lambda: np.concatenate([rng.normal(0.3, 0.6, 4), [rng.normal(0.5, 0.5)]])
127    run(np.concatenate([np.zeros(4), [0.1], np.zeros(4), [0.1], np.zeros(4), [1.0], np.zeros(4), [1.0]]))
128    for _ in range(n_restarts):
129        run(np.concatenate([num(), num(), den(), den()]))
130    return best
131
132
133def main():
134    df, AVG_QG, AVG_N, g, N, Y = load()
135    n_rows = len(df)
136    tr, va = canonical_split(n_rows)
137    print(f"rows={n_rows} train={tr.sum()} valid={va.sum()} AVG_QG={AVG_QG:.6f}")
138
139    params = {}
140    for nm in FIT_FLUXES:
141        Z = fit_flux(g, N, Y[nm], tr, DELTA[nm], N_RESTARTS, seed=abs(hash(nm)) % 997 + 1)
142        params[nm] = Z
143        print(f"  fit {nm}")
144
145    def predict_all(mask):
146        gg, NN = jnp.asarray(g[mask]), jnp.asarray(N[mask])
147        out = {"q_glc": -g[mask] * AVG_QG}
148        for nm in FIT_FLUXES:
149            out[nm] = np.asarray(predict(jnp.asarray(params[nm]), gg, NN, DELTA[nm]))
150        return out
151
152    pv = predict_all(va)
153    print("\n" + "=" * 70)
154    print(f"{'flux':5} | {'R2_all':>8} {'NMAE_all':>9} {'R2_low10':>9} {'NMAE_low10':>11}")
155    print("-" * 70)
156    for nm in FIT_FLUXES:
157        y = Y[nm][va]; p = pv[nm]
158        thr = np.percentile(np.abs(y), 10); m = np.abs(y) <= thr
159        print(f"{nm:5} | {r2(y, p):8.4f} {nmae(y, p):9.4f} {r2(y[m], p[m]):9.4f} {nmae(y[m], p[m]):11.4f}")
160
161    # Boundedness certificate over the physical sampling box.
162    rng2 = np.random.default_rng(0); NB = 200000
163    BOX_LO = np.array([0.5, 0.0, 0.0, 0.0, 0.0])
164    BOX_HI = np.array([20.0, 2.0, 2.0, 2.0, 2.0])
165    U = rng2.uniform(size=(NB, 5)) * (BOX_HI - BOX_LO) + BOX_LO
166    gb = jnp.asarray(U[:, 0] / AVG_QG); Nb = jnp.asarray(U[:, 1:5] / AVG_N)
167    print("-" * 70)
168    print(f"{'flux':5} | {'max|flux| box':>14} {'max|flux| data':>15} {'overshoot':>11}")
169    min_den = np.inf
170    all_ok = True
171    for nm in FIT_FLUXES:
172        Z, d = jnp.asarray(params[nm]), DELTA[nm]
173        dd = np.asarray((pos(aff(Z[10:15], Nb)) + d) * (pos(aff(Z[15:20], Nb)) + d))
174        min_den = min(min_den, float(dd.min()))
175        m_box = float(np.max(np.abs(np.asarray(predict(Z, gb, Nb, d)))))
176        m_dat = float(np.max(np.abs(Y[nm])))
177        ratio = m_box / m_dat if m_dat > 0 else np.inf
178        ok = ratio <= 3.0
179        all_ok &= ok
180        print(f"{nm:5} | {m_box:14.1f} {m_dat:15.1f} {ratio:10.1f}x | {'OK' if ok else 'MISS'}")
181    print("-" * 70)
182    print(f"pole-free: min denominator over box = {min_den:.5f}")
183    print(f"ALL PASS: {all_ok}")
184
185    np.savez("02_surrogate_params.npz", AVG_QG=AVG_QG, AVG_N=AVG_N,
186             DELTA=np.array([DELTA[k] for k in FIT_FLUXES]),
187             fit_fluxes=np.array(FIT_FLUXES), **{f"Z_{k}": params[k] for k in FIT_FLUXES})
188
189    def _fac(w):
190        names = ["n_X", "n_M", "n_A", "n_S"]
191        terms = [f"{w[j]:+.8g}*{names[j]}" for j in range(4)] + [f"{w[4]:+.8g}"]
192        return " ".join(terms).lstrip("+").strip()
193
194    lines = [
195        "qG  = x_data[0]", "n_X = x_data[1]", "n_M = x_data[2]",
196        "n_A = x_data[3]", "n_S = x_data[4]",
197        "pos = lambda B: 0.5 * (B + jnp.sqrt(B * B + 1.5))",
198        f"q_glc = {-AVG_QG:.8f} * qG",
199    ]
200    for nm in FIT_FLUXES:
201        Z, d = params[nm], DELTA[nm]
202        lines.append(
203            f"{nm:4s} = qG * ({_fac(Z[0:5])}) * ({_fac(Z[5:10])})"
204            f" / ((pos({_fac(Z[10:15])}) + {d}) * (pos({_fac(Z[15:20])}) + {d}))"
205        )
206    body = "\n".join(lines)
207    with open("02_surrogate_body.txt", "w") as f:
208        f.write(body + "\n")
209    print("\n" + body)
210    return all_ok

Validation R² ≥ 0.999 on all four fitted fluxes; boundedness certificate passed (max overshoot 1.7× over the sampling box, min denominator 0.199 > 0). Neither script runs as part of building these docs: solving 10,000 LPs takes a couple of minutes and needs cobra, which every doc build should not pay for. The coefficients are frozen into surrogate_fba above.

Gotchas¶

  • The surrogate is frozen, never trained. Only the two MLPs predicting its inputs are trainable_field()s; surrogate_fba itself has no learnable parameters at all.

  • A surrogate that looks accurate on validation data can still be dangerous. The boundedness certificate, not validation R² alone, is what predicts whether training will stay stable: an unconstrained fit can free-run to far outside the training data’s range in the sparse corners of a 5-dimensional sampling box.

  • _bounded_softplus keeps the MLPs inside the surrogate’s fitted range. Removing it does not error immediately: the surrogate just starts extrapolating into territory the boundedness certificate never checked.

  • n_S fixed at 0.0 is a real modeling choice, not a placeholder. It means this reaction module can never represent a deliberate product, by construction.
