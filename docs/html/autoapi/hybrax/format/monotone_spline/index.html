hybrax.format.monotone_spline¶

Adaptive monotone cubic spline fitting under a root-mean-square error budget.

The fit is a clamped cubic B-spline in normalized coordinates x = (t - t0) / T and z = (y - y0) / D, where D is the signed net change. Coefficients are anchored at 0 and 1 and strictly increasing, which makes the curve monotone in the direction of D (a sufficient, not necessary, condition).

Knots are chosen by refinement: for the current knots, a second-order cone program finds the smallest achievable RMS error, and new knots go where the squared residuals of that fit concentrate. An unconstrained pilot guides each insertion using its curvature variation, with a knot target set by max_knots. Once the budget is met, a QP minimizes the integrated squared second derivative among curves with ordered B-spline coefficients within the budget. This minimization is numerical: with strongly clustered samples the result can be rougher than necessary. All solves use Clarabel in normalized coefficients, with the RMS constraint scaled by the budget so that its tolerance stays relative to it.

Module Contents¶

hybrax.format.monotone_spline.fit_monotone_timeseries_spline(ts, *, rms_tol, increasing, max_knots=None)[source]¶

Fit a smooth monotone C2 cubic spline with automatically chosen knots.

The root-mean-square error at the samples is at most rms_tol: sqrt(mean((f(t_i) - y_i) ** 2)) <= rms_tol, in the units of the values. Both endpoints are reproduced exactly up to roundoff, so the derivative integrates to the measured net change. The requested direction must agree with the endpoints. For unequal endpoints, the derivative is strictly positive or negative everywhere in [t[0], t[-1]]. Consecutive B-spline coefficients differ by at least 1e-9 of the net change, so flat stretches get a negligible nonzero rate, about 1e-9 of the net change per knot interval. Equal endpoints give a constant curve for either direction, accepted only if it meets the budget.

Knots are added where the residuals concentrate until the budget can be met. An unconstrained pilot guides their locations throughout refinement. It uses the same RMS target and max_knots as its interior-knot target, but can miss that RMS target when its layout is too small. It only proposes locations; the returned monotone curve must meet the requested budget. For the chosen knots, the smoothing step numerically minimizes the integrated squared second derivative among curves with ordered B-spline coefficients within the budget, so the fit generally uses most of the budget and smooths noise and short reversals of direction. The error at individual samples can exceed rms_tol. Raw samples are kept unchanged.

The budget, monotonicity, continuity and endpoints are checked on the stored curve: the function either returns a curve that meets them or raises. Smoothness is not checked: with strongly clustered samples, such as bursts of fast logging, the smoothing can fail or return a curve rougher than necessary. Thinning redundant burst readings helps, but keep enough readings to resolve short additions; the budget then covers the remaining samples.

Larger rms_tol gives a smoother curve and rate; it is the smoothing knob. Choose it at least about 1.5 times the measurement noise standard deviation. At about the noise standard deviation, the fit is unstable: it can switch between few knots, the cap and an error. Every sample counts equally, so densely logged stretches weigh more towards rms_tol.

Parameters:

  • ts (hybrax.format.time_series.TimeSeries) – Scalar series with at least two samples and no jumps. Any existing spline state is ignored and replaced. Times must lie within 1000 durations of zero, so pass time since start rather than absolute timestamps.

  • rms_tol (float) – RMS error budget, > 0.

  • increasing (bool) – Whether the curve must increase or decrease.

  • max_knots (int | None) – Maximum number of interior knots. Defaults to a tenth of the number of samples, but at least 64. Also sets the pilot knot target.

Returns:

A new TimeSeries with the same samples and continuity side, holding the fitted spline. Input metadata is discarded. Fit details are stored under the “monotone_fit” metadata key.

Raises:

  • TypeError – If increasing is not a bool.

  • ValueError – For invalid input, or if the endpoints are equal and the constant curve misses the budget, or the endpoints contradict increasing.

  • RuntimeError – If the closest monotone sequence exceeds the RMS budget, if refinement reaches max_knots or cannot place further knots before the budget can be met, or if a solve or final check fails.

Return type:

hybrax.format.time_series.TimeSeries
